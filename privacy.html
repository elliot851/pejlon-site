Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Pejlon is a tool for managing Meta ad accounts. To do that it needs access to advertising data and it needs to know who you are. This page says exactly what that means: what is stored, why, where it lives, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. It is written to be read.

Who is responsible

Westros Digital Retail is the data controller for personal data processed in Pejlon. Reach us at elliot@westrosdigitalretail.se for anything on this page, including deletion requests.

Where Pejlon processes advertising data on your behalf, we act as a data processor and you remain the controller of that data.

What we do not store

  • No card details. Card numbers never reach Pejlon. They go to Stripe, and we only ever see whether a subscription is active.
  • No passwords in readable form. Only a hash, which cannot be reversed.
  • No tracking of your browsing. There is no advertising pixel, no analytics script and no third party cookies, neither in the tool nor on this website.
  • No customer data from your shop. Pejlon reads advertising figures from Meta. It does not connect to your store and never sees your customers.

Encryption

Access tokens are the most sensitive thing Pejlon holds, because they reach your ad account directly. They are never written in readable form.

  • Each token is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a random data key.
  • That data key is itself wrapped with a key derived from your password using scrypt. It exists in readable form only in memory, only while you are signed in.
  • If the data file is copied without the key, the tokens in it are useless.

We are equally clear about the limit: encryption protects a file that leaks on its own. It does not protect a machine that an attacker already controls. Full disk encryption and a locked screen do more for you there than any amount of code we can write.

Meta

Connecting Meta is done through Meta's own sign in. You approve the access in their dialog and can withdraw it at any time under Business settings at Meta, or by disconnecting inside Pejlon.

We ask for these permissions and nothing else:

PermissionWhat it is for
ads_readRead campaigns, ad sets, ads and results.
ads_managementMake the changes you ask for: budgets, status, new ads.
business_managementList ad accounts that sit inside a business portfolio.
pages_show_list, pages_manage_adsCreate ads. An ad is published as a post from a Facebook Page, so it cannot be done without these.
instagram_basicLet an ad run on Instagram as well as Facebook.

Pejlon never posts organic content, never messages anyone, and never reads your inbox or your followers.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you do, we request a single scope, drive.readonly, plus your email address so we can show which Google account is connected.

Read only means read only: Pejlon cannot change, move, share or delete anything in your Drive. Files you select are downloaded into memory, sent straight to your ad account, and released. Nothing from your Drive is written to disk by Pejlon, and nothing is kept after the transfer.

Pejlon's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google Drive data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not let humans read it except where you ask us to for support, or where the law requires it.

Disconnect at any time inside Pejlon, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. Card details are entered on Stripe's own page and never pass through Pejlon. What we hold is a customer reference, the subscription status, and the billing dates.

Stripe processes this data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. Invoices are retained for seven years as Swedish bookkeeping law requires, and that retention overrides a deletion request for those records.

Where the data lives

Pejlon runs on the customer's own machine or on a server the customer controls. The data file sits there and nowhere else. There is no central Pejlon database holding customers' advertising data.

The only outbound connections the tool makes are these, and there are no others:

  • Meta's Graph API, to read figures and make the changes you ask for.
  • Google Drive, only if you connect it, and only to list and fetch the files you pick.
  • Stripe, for the subscription.
  • A public exchange rate source, to convert cost of goods entered in another currency. No personal data is sent, only a currency pair.

Meta, Google and Stripe are US companies and may process data outside the EU. Transfers rest on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses where that framework does not apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have data erased, except where the law requires us to keep it
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent, where processing rests on consent

Write to elliot@westrosdigitalretail.se. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY.

Deleting is genuinely deleting. When an account is removed, its tokens, sessions and personal details go with it. Because access tokens are encrypted under a key derived from your password, they become unreadable the moment that key is gone, regardless of what any backup might still contain.

Cookies

Pejlon uses no cookies. Your sign in session is kept in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device and is not sent to anyone but the Pejlon server you signed in to. This website sets no cookies at all, which is why you are not being asked to accept any.

Security incidents

If a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we notify you and the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. We will say what happened, what was affected and what to do, without waiting until we have a complete picture.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we say so before the change takes effect rather than quietly updating the date at the top. The date under the heading is the last revision.
